## Current source review

The directly retrieved [Angel One tariff](https://www.angelone.in/exchange-transaction-charges) corroborates the draft brokerage, venue transaction fees, sell-side STT, stamp duty, SEBI, GST and published clearing assumptions checked here. It lists IPFT explicitly for NSE; absence of an explicit BSE value does not establish a zero BFO component. The tariff does not establish the proposed contract-note rounding/allocation rules. Another Angel One-owned pricing page differs in its displayed NSE transaction rate, reinforcing use of the directly retrieved current tariff rather than an old indexed snippet. No rates or review flags were changed.

SmartAPI's indexed [market-data documentation](https://smartapi.angelone.in/docs/MarketData) describes depth quantity as a quantity field. The official [SnapQuote SDK parser](https://github.com/angel-one/smartapi-python/blob/main/SmartApi/smartWebSocketV2.py) establishes field layout and byte types; that alone does not establish lots-versus-units semantics. Direct documentation requests returned an authorization page in this audit, so indexed text was not represented as a successful authenticated retrieval. Neither order-quantity documentation nor a different broker's feed is sufficient to close E01.

The official [SmartConnect SDK](https://github.com/angel-one/smartapi-python/blob/main/SmartApi/smartConnect.py) declares historical candles, historical OI and brokerage estimation routes. These are discovery evidence for follow-up, not proof of account entitlement, retention rights, quotas or contract-note equivalence. SWAN currently does not allowlist those routes. A future read-only charge-estimate comparison may help investigate rounding, but must not be represented as conclusive proof of session-level allocation.
