## 16 November 2026: free provider decision (supersedes earlier AlgoTest-first recommendation)

The user requires a free provider and autonomous integration for NIFTY/SENSEX options. Do not purchase AlgoTest or another paid service for this phase. MegaBull is the only one of these two candidates with a publicly documented paper-order API and is the first free account-level candidate to verify. It is not yet approved as SWAN's paper execution backend: its public materials conflict on whether options/API access remains free, and its public API contract does not document an options-chain endpoint, streaming quotes, partial fills, or a realistic fill model. Use only after the free account actually demonstrates the complete required contract; if that fails, keep research open rather than silently substituting paid or prohibited automation.

Thepapertrade is explicitly rejected for autonomous integration. Its current terms prohibit robots/scripts from accessing it, placing simulated orders, or extracting data. It has no public automation API contract found in this review. It can be used manually by the user as a visual comparison desk, but do not scrape, automate, or feed its private UI/data into SWAN.

### MegaBull fact check

- Its first-party site and API docs claim free API keys and simulated orders, positions, holdings, market-watch and reports. API key is created in the user's profile; documented base URL is `https://api.megabull.in`; requests use the `api-key` header. The key expires monthly.
- Its published contract includes NFO/BFO option segments, expiry, lot-size and tick-size fields, and order endpoints with BUY/SELL, MKT/LIMIT/SL, MIS/CNC, plus order/position readback. This establishes an API-shaped integration route for options instruments, but not full agent-readiness.
- The API contract exposes an LTP endpoint, not a documented option-chain, bid/ask/depth or WebSocket feed. Instrument lookup documentation describes a CSV and a small set of columns, while the OpenAPI market-watch schema describes richer metadata. The public CSV URL was not accessible during this review (403), so contract selection/mapping must be verified from a free account before coding this as reliable.
- The order documentation states fills are all-or-nothing, MKT fills at the last traded price, LIMIT/SL can remain pending, and there is no partial-fill state. That is materially weaker than Angel One execution for scalping and cannot validate queue, liquidity, or partial-fill behavior.
- Free-tier wording conflicts: homepage/FAQ describe ₹5 lakh virtual capital, free API, and no API charges, but the FAQ says the product is free to try for seven days and only equity remains free for life. A separate payments policy says core paper trading with ₹10 lakh virtual capital and real-time data will remain free. Options/API entitlement after day seven is therefore NOT fact-checked as free. Do not enter payment details or buy a plan; check the account dashboard/API response and written provider terms first.
- “Live/real-time” data is MegaBull's own claim, not independently verified here. No market-data vendor, latency SLA, or redistribution rights were confirmed. SWAN should retain Angel One as its market-observation source and treat MegaBull-reported fills/positions as an independent simulator only.

Official sources: https://megabull.in/ ; https://megabull.in/paper-trading-api-india.html ; https://megabull.in/api/megabull-openapi.json ; https://megabull.in/main/payments-refunds.html

### thepapertrade fact check

- The first-party site says free forever, with live prices and virtual money, NIFTY/BANKNIFTY/FINNIFTY and SENSEX/BANKEX options, expiry coverage, modeled statutory costs and margin.
- Its About page says its broker connection is read-only, it has no code path to an order endpoint, and it does not execute/rout orders. Its Terms (v2, effective 28 September 2026) say prices may be delayed/interrupted/incomplete, fills are generated by its own software, modeled charges/margin differ from a user's broker, and it makes no uptime/latency/data-retention promise.
- Most decisively, Terms section 7 prohibits robots, scrapers, scripts, automated simulated orders, and data extraction. No public API/webhook was found. Therefore it is not a permissible SWAN agent integration absent explicit written permission and a supported official API; do not reverse-engineer its web client or automate the UI.
- The Terms state accounts are for personal non-commercial use and data remains subject to third-party restrictions. Do not redistribute its market data or expose it in SWAN.

Official sources: https://thepapertrade.com/ ; https://thepapertrade.com/about ; https://thepapertrade.com/terms ; https://thepapertrade.com/privacy

A simulator cannot validate actual Angel One latency, queue position, partial fills, rejections, or margin behavior.

## What the observation period must establish

The objective is to assess agent/process reliability, not to declare a profitable strategy from a short sample. Log each actual Angel One quote and evidence snapshot, agent analysis/proposal/review with strategy/prompt/model versions, deterministic risk decision, provider request/response ID, provider fill/exit and timestamps, and every pause/error/reconnect. Evaluate evidence freshness and citation validity, correct contract/side/expiry/lot, forbidden-trade/risk-rule violations, duplicate or missing entries/exits, quote-to-fill differences, fill delay, charges, daily drawdown, and net outcomes. Preserve WAIT/no-trade decisions too. Segment results by version and market condition; never pool outcomes after changing the agents or rules.

An external paper provider's fill is one observation of how its simulator handled the order. Compare it to the Angel One bid/ask/depth and later tradable quotes saved at the decision/exit times. It cannot validate real Angel One queue position, partial fills, broker rejections, margin or live latency. A one-to-two-week run can find logic and operational defects, but cannot prove that AI signals are right or ready for real capital.

Potential architecture: actual broker market observations → SWAN agents/review → SWAN risk → external paper strategy/order adapter → provider-reported paper state → reconciled SWAN journal. Provider state and locally calculated assumptions must be clearly distinguished. Keep the existing local engine available but do not assume it is the user's selected execution backend or delete it before a replacement is verified.
